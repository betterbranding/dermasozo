Brand Guidelines

Clinical Skin Recovery · Est. 2026

01 · Logo System

Two lockups, one brand

The stacked crest leads on products and packaging; the horizontal lockup leads on the website, letterheads, and documents. Never redraw, re-color, or re-typeset either mark.

Primary — Products & Packaging

Stacked Crest

Regeneration Burst crest above the wordmark: copper derma, cobalt Sozo script. On packaging the crest renders in embossed foil: Copper Gold on OPC DermMist™, sky blue on Recovery Mist™. Use on labels, cartons, and social avatars.

Primary — Web & Letterhead

Horizontal Lockup

Crest at left of the wordmark for headers, email signatures, letterheads, and any wide format.

Reversed — Dark Grounds

Reversed Stacked

For cobalt, deep teal, or other dark backgrounds. Maintain contrast; never place the standard mark on dark grounds.

Rules

Usage

✓Clearspace = height of the burst crest on all sides
✓Minimum size: 32 px crest height (stacked), 140 px width (horizontal)
✕No stretching, recoloring, drop shadows, or outlines
✕No taglines or icons locked to the mark
✕“SoZo” casing is sacred — never “Sozo” or “SOZO” in text
02 · Color

Palette

Clinical calm with a warm metallic accent. Cobalt leads authority; copper brings the premium foil warmth; teal and sky serve as cool supporting accents. Copper Gold is the production pump-cap color, sampled from the physical satin-gold overcap, and it carries the OPC DermMist™ foil crest and accents so label and cap read as one metal.

Deep Teal#0B6E6E · Cool accentPMS 7717 C · C95 M40 Y56 K21
Sky Blue#CAEFFF · Highlights / cool accentsPMS 290 C · C18 M0 Y0 K0

Print specs: Pantone references are the nearest Solid Coated matches, and CMYK builds target coated stock via ICC print profiles. Deep cobalt sits at the edge of the CMYK gamut, so always proof against a physical Pantone guide before production. For foil stamping, Copper pairs naturally with metallic PMS 876 C. Copper Gold was measured from photographs of the production pump cap under neutral-balanced light (Lab 58.5 / 12.6 / 30.8); PMS 729 C is the nearest flat ink and Premium Metallic PMS 10131 C the nearest metallic. Anodized metal cannot be reproduced exactly by ink or foil, so match any Copper Gold foil or print to the physical cap, not to a screen.

04 · Packaging

Recovery Mist™ label

Satin cobalt ground with the embossed sky-blue foil crest (sky burst in its cell ring, foil derma, white Sozo), Recovery Mist™ in white DM Serif, SKIN RECOVERY SYSTEM in tracked sky-blue caps over a sky-blue rule, and a sky-blue PAIN RELIEF callout pill in cobalt lettering above the net contents. The UPC prints as a bare white code and the QR as a crisp navy code, both aligned flush to the Drug Facts panel edges. Drug Facts panel follows FDA OTC format on white.

Production note: this label prints on a translucent matte frosted film applied to a cobalt blue bottle. The cobalt ground in the artwork is the bottle itself showing through the frosted label.

06 · In the World

Product photography

Frosted cobalt bottles on marble spa surfaces, soft daylight. Recovery Mist™ wears the sky-blue foil crest; OPC DermMist™ wears the copper foil crest; both bottles carry the metal spray top. The production overcap is satin gold (Copper Gold, #AE8457); renders showing a brushed copper top predate the cap sample and will be refreshed. The burst emblem always keeps its enclosing ring, a skin cell rendered in foil. Shot together, the sky and copper labels read as the two-step system at a glance.

07 · Voice

How DermaSoZo speaks

The customer is the hero

StoryBrand framing: she wants her skin back. DermaSoZo is the calm, clinical guide with a proven 2-step system. We never make the brand the hero.

CAFE cadence

Captivate with the moment of damage, agitate the cost of waiting, fascinate with the recovery story, educate with the clinical why.

Tone

Warm authority. Short sentences. Evidence over hype. “Recover Beautifully.” is the promise — clinical results, beautifully delivered.

Vocabulary

Say: recovery, clinical-grade, protocol, barrier. Avoid: miracle, cure, anti-aging clichés, fear-based burn language on-pack.
