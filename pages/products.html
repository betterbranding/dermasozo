Two products. One clear job each. Recovery Mist™ meets the moment after a procedure when skin is uncomfortable. OPC DermMist™ supports recovery every night after. Here's exactly what's in each bottle, what it does, and why the order matters.

Step 01 · Comfort First

Recovery Mist™

Recovery Mist™ is the bottle you reach for the moment treated skin feels uncomfortable: tight, flushed, or tender after a procedure. It's an OTC topical analgesic, formulated to the FDA OTC monograph standard and delivered as a fine mist spray, so you never have to rub anything into tender skin.

Active ingredient: Lidocaine HCl 2 percent, a topical analgesic that promptly relieves discomfort and can be reapplied as needed throughout recovery. It's one of the most widely used topical analgesics in medicine.

A mist so fine it is easily absorbed, and irritation is relieved. Point, mist, and let it do its work.

Step 02 · Active Restoring

OPC DermMist™

Once Recovery Mist™ has absorbed, OPC DermMist™ picks up where it left off. It's an antioxidant mist built around OPCs, oligomeric proanthocyanidins, a class of botanical antioxidants found in grape seed and other plant sources, recognized for decades in skin science.

OPC DermMist™ helps defend against free radical damage and helps to restore healthy skin. It's cosmetic, not a drug, and it knows its job: a nighttime antioxidant application for continued recovery, night after night.

Light enough to layer invisibly under skin care products. It disappears into your routine instead of adding another step you have to think about.

Which One, When

Two products. Two very different jobs.

You don't have to guess which bottle to reach for. Here's the simple version.

Recovery Mist™

Reach for this the moment treated skin feels uncomfortable: tight, flushed, or tender right after a procedure. It's the first response, built for the first hours and days of recovery.

OPC DermMist™

Reach for this every night through recovery: the redness, the tightness, the "still not quite back to normal" phase. It's the nighttime antioxidant application that keeps showing up after discomfort fades, and it's just as at home in a daily routine.

They're built to be used together. Recovery Mist™ meets the moment. OPC DermMist™ meets the days that follow. Separately, they work. Together, they're the system, and neither one is trying to do the other's job.
