Two products. One clear job each. Recovery Mist™ meets the moment your skin is uncomfortable. OPC DermMist™ supports it every day after. Here's exactly what's in each bottle, what it does, and why the order matters.

Step 01 · Comfort First

Recovery Mist™

Recovery Mist™ is the bottle you reach for the moment skin feels uncomfortable: sun-stressed, flushed, or freshly out of a procedure chair. It's an OTC topical analgesic, held to the same regulatory bar as the pharmacy staples you already trust, formulated as a fine, continuous spray so you never have to rub anything into tender skin.

Active ingredient: Lidocaine HCl 2 percent, a topical analgesic that temporarily relieves discomfort right where you spray it. It's a well-studied, widely used ingredient, not a novel formula with an unproven track record.

No pressing. No rubbing. Just point, mist, and let it do its work while you get on with your day.

Step 02 · Active Restoring

OPC DermMist™

Once Recovery Mist™ has absorbed, OPC DermMist™ picks up where it left off. It's a weightless antioxidant mist built around OPCs, oligomeric proanthocyanidins, a class of botanical antioxidants found in grape seed and other plant sources that has been studied in skin science for decades.

OPC DermMist™ supports the look of even, healthy skin and supports skin's natural recovery process. It's cosmetic, not a drug, and it knows its job: help skin look calm, hydrated, and cared for, day after day, whether or not anything went wrong that week.

Weightless enough to layer invisibly under the rest of your skincare, and under SPF. It disappears into your routine instead of adding another step you have to think about.

Which One, When

Two products. Two very different jobs.

You don't have to guess which bottle to reach for. Here's the simple version.

Recovery Mist™

Reach for this the moment skin feels uncomfortable: tight, flushed, freshly sun-stressed, or right after a procedure your physician is guiding you through. It's the first response, not the daily habit, built for the moment things go a little sideways.

OPC DermMist™

Reach for this every day skin still looks stressed, through the redness, the tightness, the "still not quite back to normal" phase. It's the daily support that keeps showing up after the discomfort fades, and it's just as at home in a regular routine.

They're built to be used together. Recovery Mist™ meets the moment. OPC DermMist™ meets the days that follow. Separately, they work. Together, they're the system, and neither one is trying to do the other's job.
