Questions, Answered

Everything you're wondering, answered.

Straight answers, no fine print you have to hunt for.

How often can I apply Recovery Mist™?

Follow the directions on your product label. Most people reapply as needed throughout the day for comfort, and there's no need to wait a set number of hours between mists. Stop use and ask a doctor if your skin worsens or discomfort lasts more than seven days.

Can I use it on my face?

Yes, it's intended for external use on the face and body. Avoid the eyes and mucous membranes, and always follow the directions on the label. The no-touch mist makes facial application easier than a cream or gel you'd otherwise have to rub in.

Is it safe for kids?

Recovery Mist™ is intended for use as directed on the label. For children under 12, ask a doctor before use. When in doubt, a quick call to your pediatrician or pharmacist is always the right move.

Does it sting?

It shouldn't. The fine, continuous mist applies without rubbing or pressing, so there's no friction on tender skin. Most people feel comfort first, not sting, which is exactly why it's built as a mist instead of a rub-in gel.

When do I use OPC DermMist™?

Right after Recovery Mist™ absorbs, typically a minute or two after you apply it. From there, it layers invisibly underneath your usual skincare and SPF, and it's gentle enough to become a daily habit rather than an occasional rescue product.

Does this replace sunscreen?

No. OPC DermMist™ supports the look of healthy, resilient skin, but it isn't a sunscreen and doesn't replace one. Keep using the SPF your physician recommends, or ask about our SPF 50 companion. Think of it as support for your skin, not a substitute for protecting it.
