Skin wants to recover. We help.

DermaSoZo™ started with an uncomfortable observation: the products marketed as "after-sun care" hadn't meaningfully changed in decades. We thought skin under stress deserved a system built to a higher standard, so we built one.

Where We Started

We watched "after-sun care" stay stuck in 1985.

Walk down any drugstore aisle and you'll find the same after-sun shelf that existed when your mother was your age: a wall of aloe vera gel in a dozen different bottles, all doing roughly the same thing, which is to say, not very much. It cools. It's wet. Within an hour, it's gone, and you're exactly where you started, reaching for the same bottle tomorrow.

We didn't think that was good enough, not for a category that touches so many people who genuinely spend time outdoors, in a treatment room, on a sideline, on a plane home from somewhere sunny. Comfort care for stressed skin deserved the same clinical rigor as anything else in a routine.

So we set out to build it: a topical analgesic formulated to the FDA OTC monograph standard, paired with an antioxidant mist grounded in real ingredient science, in a two-step ritual simple enough to actually stick to.

We spent more time in ingredient literature than in focus groups. Lidocaine HCl earned its place because it's one of the most established topical analgesics in medicine, not because it tested well in a room full of strangers. OPCs earned theirs because the antioxidant research is real, even if our label copy has to stay modest about what "supports the look of" actually means.

The name is a small nod to an old word, sozo, the idea of being made whole again. That's the promise we're chasing, one mist at a time, not a claim we print on a label and hope nobody checks.

What We Stand For

Three things we won't compromise.

These aren't aspirations printed on a wall. They're the rules behind every formula, every label, and every claim we make.

Clinical Honesty

We say what's proven and nothing more. Recovery Mist™ stays within OTC monograph language. OPC DermMist™ stays in cosmetic language. No borrowed medical claims, ever. If a claim can't survive a label review, it doesn't survive our marketing meeting either.

Effortless Ritual

Recovery shouldn't require a ten-step routine or a chemistry degree. Two mists, in order, and you're done. That's the whole ritual. We'd rather build one system you actually finish than eleven products you eventually abandon under the sink.

Respect for Your Intelligence

No miracle language, no fear tactics, no fine print doing the opposite of what the headline promises. You've read enough labels to know the difference, and so do we. We'd rather earn a second purchase with honesty than a first one with hype.

Recover Beautifully

Restoration isn't a claim we print on a label. It's the whole reason we started.

Where We're Based

Founded in Texas. Formulated for everywhere the sun shines.

DermaSoZo™ is based in Addison, Texas, which means our team knows exactly what an actual Texas summer does to skin. It isn't an abstraction to us, it's the view out the window from about May through October.

We work with a small, deliberately un-bloated team: people who read ingredient studies for fun, argue about label copy longer than is probably healthy, and believe a two-product system beats an eleven-product shelf every time.

Today, DermaSoZo™ partners with dermatology practices and med spas that dispense the Recovery Kit™ as part of their own post-procedure protocols, alongside everyday use for anyone whose skin has had a long day outside. Same two products, same standards, two very different mornings.
