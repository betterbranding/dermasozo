The take-home protocol your patients follow.

Every treatment ends the same way: your patient goes home. What happens over the next few days shapes how they feel about their results, and about you. The Derma SoZo™ Recovery Kit™ is a two-step, physician-directed protocol simple enough that patients actually stick with it.

The Post-Procedure Gap

You finish the treatment. Then what?

You control every variable in the room: energy settings, technique, aftercare instructions delivered clearly and calmly. Then your patient walks out the door, and control transfers to them.

That's usually where the plan quietly falls apart. Half the instructions are forgotten by the time they reach the parking lot. They reach for whatever's under the bathroom sink, a leftover sample, a drugstore moisturizer, sometimes nothing at all. Comfort drops. Redness lingers longer than it needs to. Their opinion of your results starts forming around how their skin felt on day two, not how precise your technique was on day one.

A great outcome that feels uncomfortable at home still reads as a disappointing one to the patient.

The DermaSoZo™ Recovery Kit™ closes that gap. Two touch-free mists, Recovery Mist™ and OPC DermMist™, built for exactly this window: comfort your patient can apply without rubbing tender, treated skin, directed by you instead of discovered by them in a random aisle.
